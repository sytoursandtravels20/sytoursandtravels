OUR PLACE

Goa is better when you can explore it at your own pace.

SY Tours & Travels is based in Panjim and helps visitors find practical ways to experience Goa. Choose a car or bike for the road, book a water activity, or plan time aboard a boat or yacht.

We bring these options together so you can spend less time coordinating and more time enjoying the coast, the towns, and the moments in between.
