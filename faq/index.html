Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about exploring Goa with SY Tours & Travels.

What services does SY Tours & Travels offer?

SY Tours & Travels offers car rentals, scooty rentals, taxi services, water activities, luxury yacht bookings, and boat cruises in Goa.

Do I need a valid driving licence to rent a car or scooty?

Yes, a valid driving licence is required for self-drive vehicle rentals. Please ensure your licence meets the applicable requirements and remains valid throughout the rental period.

Are there extra charges for night pickups or drops?

Extra charges may apply for pickups or drops between 10 PM and 7 AM, depending on the location.

Is there a security deposit for rentals?

Yes, a refundable security deposit is required. The amount varies by car category: Economy (₹3,000), Premium (₹5,000), and Luxury (starting from ₹10,000).

Can I use the rented car outside Goa?

No, our rental services are currently limited to within Goa state boundaries only.

What is the minimum rental period?

There’s no minimum rental period. You can rent a car or scooty for just one day or longer. One-day rentals have a slightly higher daily rate, while longer bookings offer better value. We’re happy to help you find the best option for your Goa trip.

What is your rental timing system?

We follow a 9:00 AM to 9:00 AM rental system. Late returns after 9:00 AM may attract an additional day charge.

Can I extend my booking?

Extensions are subject to availability. Please contact us 24 hours in advance. Rates may vary.

What about damages?

The customer is liable for any damage during the rental period.

Still have a question?

Contact our team and we’ll be happy to help with your Goa trip.
