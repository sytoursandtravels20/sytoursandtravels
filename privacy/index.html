Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This page explains what information you may share when using the SY Tours & Travels website and how it is used to respond to enquiries.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and message. Phone number is optional. Please do not send payment details or other sensitive information through the form.

How enquiries are handled

Contact form submissions are sent through Google Apps Script to SY Tours & Travels so the team can reply and help with your enquiry. Messages may be retained in email or related systems while they are needed to respond or manage the enquiry.

Third-party services

This site loads resources and content from services including Google Maps, Google Fonts, Google Apps Script, Blogger, jsDelivr, and Unsplash. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

Cookies and analytics

The site does not currently include its own analytics or advertising trackers. Embedded maps and other third-party services may use their own technologies.

Your questions

For questions about a contact enquiry or this policy, email sytourandtravels20@gmail.com.
