Scooty & Bike Rental FAQs

Answers to common questions about renting a scooty or bike in Goa.

Do I need a valid driving licence to rent a scooty or bike?

Yes, a valid driving licence is required for rental vehicles. Please ensure your licence meets the applicable requirements and remains valid throughout the rental period.

Are there extra charges for night pickups or drops?

Extra charges may apply for pickups or drops between 10 PM and 7 AM, depending on the location.

Is there a security deposit for scooty or bike rentals?

A refundable security deposit may be required. The amount depends on the vehicle; please confirm it when enquiring.

Can I use the rented scooty or bike outside Goa?

No, our rental services are currently limited to within Goa state boundaries only.

What is the rental timing system?

We follow a 9:00 AM to 9:00 AM rental system. Late returns after 9:00 AM may attract an additional day charge.
